# PKG-1 "Times and MathTime, perfected"

## 1. Name and philosophy

PKG-1 keeps what TJO already prints (Times LT Std, MathTime Professional 2, Whitney) and fixes what the
design-research lane measured as wrong with it: no protrusion, a link colour below WCAG AA, three
figure colours of the same lightness, a 91-character line. It adds his talk identity (Whitney plus the
re-stepped dark teal of `presentations/STYLE_GUIDE.md` System A) so that the book, his slides and the
web pages read as one author. It optimises for the printed page. It refuses to compromise the print
math for the screen: on screen it uses the closest OpenType relative (STIX Two) and states the
difference instead of hiding it. Lineage: the APS/Elsevier journal look that MathTime defined
(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MathTime), MTW's marginal dependency statements
(https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitation_(book); layered-docs/REPORT.md section 1.1), the Distill
gutter (https://distill.pub/guide/) for the margin column, and TJO's five commandments (one idea per
block, no decoration).

## 2. Math-font decision

Option (b) of design-research/REPORT.md section 2.3: **Times LT Std + MTPro2 in print; STIX Two Text +
STIX Two Math on screen.** Rule that limits the mismatch to one boundary:

| Rendered through | Text / math | Media |
|---|---|---|
| TeX, offline | Times LT Std + MTPro2 (Type 1) | print PDF, TikZ/pgfplots figures, equation sprites for video |
| Browser or game engine, live | STIX Two Text + STIX Two Math (OFL) | HTML chapters (MathML), demos, games |

So paper and every TeX-rendered artefact match exactly; live screen text matches only in family.
Measured differences (specimens/detail-eq.png, first versus third strip; pkg1-1.png versus
pkg1-html.png): STIX Two has the larger x-height and darker colour; MTPro2 sets wider (a 114 mm
measure produced a 19.5 pt overfull line on one inline formula that STIX set without overflow);
the italic Greek differs most (rho, epsilon, hbar). The current STIX Two on this machine is 2.12
(TeX Live 2023); upstream is 2.14 (https://github.com/stipub/stixfonts/releases/tag/v2.14).

Two hard consequences, both measured on `specimens/pkg1.pdf`:
- **No Unicode for the math in the PDF.** `pdftotext` returns "Dichteoperator   0" for
  "Dichteoperator rho >= 0" and "D" for "=". MTPro2's Type 1 fonts carry no usable ToUnicode map,
  so copy-paste, search and screen readers fail on every formula; tagged PDF for math is impossible.
  The STIX specimen (pkg3.pdf) returns "𝜌 ≥ 0". This is the price of the package.
- **No Typst.** Typst needs an OpenType MATH font; PKG-1 stays on XeLaTeX until `mathtime-otf`
  (scratchpad `gh/mathtime-otf/PRD.md`, PRD stage) produces one and PCTeX's licence allows its use.
  If that happens, the screen stack switches from STIX Two Math to MathTime-OTF and the mismatch ends.

Quantum-number ladder n = 1..4 (single hue, monotone lightness, min dL 0.16): print/screen-light
`#013050`, `#055F89`, `#3792B5`, `#73C6D9`; screen-dark/video (n = 1 lightest) `#B1E9F7`, `#6DBDDC`,
`#3C90B8`, `#24648B`. More than four levels: repeat the ramp with dashes.

Physics roles: state (rho, psi, Bloch vector) = state; effect, POVM element, detector axis,
observable = effect; Hamiltonian, energy, transitions, time arrows = energy; bath, reservoir,
classically forbidden region, reference curves = environment (never by colour alone: dashed or
hatched); quantum-number ladders = ladder ramp. Temperature and time use a single-hue sequential ramp
(paper to energy hue, monotone in L) and only in plots that show no energy role.

Checks (`tokens-tables.md`): body on paper 21.0:1 print, 16.9:1 screen-light, 14.8:1 dark; link on
paper 6.7:1 print, 6.5:1 screen-light (WCAG 2.2 AA needs 4.5:1, https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/);
data roles min dL 0.20 print (greyscale budget 0.15 met), min dE_OK under deuteranopia 0.237, under
protanopia 0.155. Known weakness: the energy role has 2.2:1 on white, below the 3:1 of WCAG 1.4.11;
rule: energy is used only as fills, arrows of 1.8 pt or more, or areas, always with an ink label.
In dark modes the data roles are 0.06 apart in L: dark modes do not promise greyscale survival.
Greyscale check of the printed figure: `specimens/greyscale-figures.png` (top strip).

## 6. Figure style

Line weights: axes 0.5 pt, boundaries and potentials 0.8 pt, data curves 1.1 pt, level lines 1.3 pt,
energy arrows 1.8 pt, projection and helper lines 0.4 pt dashed (1.6 on, 1.4 off), leaders 0.3 pt.
Markers: filled circles 1.9 pt radius for data on a quantised spectrum (as in `fig_vl11_kasten.tex`),
open circles for reference series. Axes: `axis lines=left` or `middle`, Stealth tips 4 pt, no box, no
grid unless values are read off. Forbidden regions: `north east lines` hatching in the environment
colour, as a band beside the boundary, never under labels. Labels: direct labels, never legends;
symbols in MTPro2 at `\small`, prose in Whitney 8 pt (Whitney has no U+2192 arrow: XeLaTeX reported
"Missing character: There is no → (U+2192) in font Whitney-Book.otf"; arrows always come from the
math font). Meaning of colour: role colours only; quantum numbers by the ladder ramp; temperature or
time by a single-hue sequential ramp; phase of a wavefunction by a cyclic map (Crameri romaO,
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.1243862). Caption rule: first sentence in bold states the message
(Doumont); the rest makes the figure readable without the text.

## 7. Interactive components

Buttons: Whitney Semibold 15 px, ink text on paper, 1 px rule border, 3 px radius; primary button
filled with accent, paper text. Slider: native range input with `accent-color` = the role it
controls (state for a state parameter). Readout: tabular figures, 1 px rule frame, value in the
language's decimal format ("p = 0,741"). Toggle: checkbox styled as a 2-state switch with the label
on the left. The depth affordance is `<details>`/`<summary>` (works without JavaScript). Focus: 2 px
link-colour outline, 2 px offset, on every control. Dark mode: the screen-dark token column,
selected by `prefers-color-scheme` with a manual override (`data-theme`), never a filter.
three.js scene: background = paper token; one hemisphere light (sky = paper, ground = rule) and one
directional key light from upper left; MeshStandardMaterial roughness 0.6, metalness 0; axis gizmo
drawn as ink lines with labels as HTML overlays (CSS2DRenderer) in STIX Two Math, on opaque paper
plates (Liquid Glass lesson, https://pxlnv.com/blog/on-liquid-glass/). Game HUD: top-left plate with
the score as a physical quantity in Whitney Semibold 16 px tabular, top-right level name, bottom
centre the controls; no avatars, no streaks.

## 8. Video style

16:9, 3840 x 2160, 30 fps (60 fps for fast dynamics). Dark ground (video token `#070D12`), because
TJO's talks use black or white grounds and the role colours were stepped to read on dark. Camera:
orthographic for 2D sequences; for 3D a fixed 40-degree vertical field of view, moves only between
explanatory viewpoints at constant angular speed, axes always visible. Title card: Whitney
Semibold 120 px, accent-dark `#8AD0EB` on the ground, nothing else. Lower thirds: Whitney Book 44 px
on an opaque plate (rule token). Equations: XeLaTeX with MTPro2 to SVG (`dvisvgm`), so video
equations are identical to print; they appear by fading in whole terms, transformations as term
morphs (manim grammar, https://github.com/3b1b/manim); no sentence on screen repeats the narration.
3D materials: state = base colour `#77C9F3`, roughness 0.5; effect = `#30C78D`; probability densities
(|psi_210|^2 for the 2p orbital) as volume renders with a sequential map from the ground to the state
hue; phase as the cyclic map. Reuse as a print figure: re-render the same scene file with the
print-light tokens on white; a dark frame is never inverted into print.

## 9. Bilingual rules

German quotes „…“ and English “…” via `csquotes` `\enquote` (the specimen uses `[autostyle]`);
decimal comma in German, point in English, through `siunitx` `locale=DE`/`UK` (axis labels and web
readouts follow the same switch); thin-space digit grouping; `ngerman` hyphenation patterns with
`\emergencystretch=1em`. Upright d, e, i and upright operator names (tr, dim) via `\dd`, `\eu`, `\iu`
(ISO 80000-2; Higham, https://nhigham.com/2016/01/28/typesetting-mathematics-according-to-the-iso-standard/);
the specimen uses `\mathrm{e}`, `\mathrm{i}`. Run-in labels switch with the build: Postulat/Postulate,
Vertiefung/Deeper, Zurück zum Kern/Back to the core, Abb./Fig. Figure prose labels go through
`\tpcifenglish{}{}`; symbols stay bare. Web pages carry `lang="de"`/`"en"` so the browser hyphenates.

## 11. Costs and risks

- Licences: Times LT Std, MTPro2 Complete and Whitney desktop are installed (owned). MTPro2 for a
  second machine: USD 179, academic USD 129 (search summary of https://pctex.com/mtpro2changes.html;
  page fetch returned HTTP 403, so [UNVERIFIED]); EUR not stated by PCTeX. Whitney webfont: H&Co
  subscription, entry tier reported as USD 199 per year for 250,000 pageviews per month
  (https://www.vendr.com/marketplace/hoeflerandco, undated, [UNVERIFIED]); app embedding for games
  [UNVERIFIED]. STIX Two: OFL, free. Whether MTPro2's licence allows rasterising equations into
  published video [UNVERIFIED, licence text not read].
- Build complexity: two math stacks (MTPro2 for TeX outputs, STIX Two for live screen), so two
  spacing regressions to watch; no Typst pilot possible; everything else as now (XeLaTeX).
- Repo changes: `tpc-style.sty` geometry (2.5 cm all round to the 22/114/6/46/22 grid), colours
  (replace cgblue/birrenred/birrengreen/munsell by the token file), `microtype`, `csquotes`, `siunitx`,
  `\emergencystretch`; `tpc-tikz-templates.sty` cycle lists; 22 figures recoloured (the hard part:
  every figure that relied on hue alone needs a second code).
- Risks: (1) the PDF's math is not machine-readable (measured above); for a public university
  document this may conflict with accessibility duties (BITV 2.0) [UNVERIFIED applicability to lecture
  notes]. (2) MTPro2 sets wide: inline formulas in a 70-character measure overflow more often
  (19.5 pt overfull in the specimen until the sentence was rewritten). (3) Whitney lacks some symbols
  (U+2192 measured); every label font fallback must be tested. (4) Web licensing of Whitney is a
  recurring cost tied to traffic.

## 12. Verdict

PKG-1 is right if the printed book is the artefact TJO will be judged by and he accepts that the web
and games use a Times relative rather than MathTime itself. It is the wrong choice if the PDF must be
accessible or built in Typst before `mathtime-otf` exists.
