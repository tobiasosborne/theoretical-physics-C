**Key findings**
1. **The math font is the binding constraint.** MTPro2 is Type 1, so it cannot be used in Typst, MathJax or native MathML.
   - STIX Two (free, a Times-like revival by Tiro Typeworks) is the only Times-family candidate that works the same in XeLaTeX, Typst, MathJax 4 and native MathML.
   - The other strong competitors are Minion Pro + Minion Math and Lucida Bright Math OT.
   - Specimen A (the current Times LT Std + MTPro2 build) runs past the 138 mm specimen width where STIX Two fits. Specimen C (TeX Gyre Termes) is the weakest.
   - Specimen E: in math, Baskerville's italic Z and N come out as swash forms.
2. **The current palette fails greyscale.** cgblue, birrenred and birrengreen have OKLCH lightness 0.53–0.55, so they print as one grey. The link colour munsell has 3.82:1 contrast on white, below WCAG AA.
   - None of the 6–7 colour palettes tested survives greyscale. Only Tol high-contrast does, with lightness 0.39 / 0.58 / 0.77.
   - So print can carry 3–4 colour roles at most. Everything else needs shape, line-style or label encoding.
3. **Repo facts.**
   - The lines are about 85–95 characters long.
   - `\dd` (upright d) is defined but used 0 times. The German lectures write italic `\,dx` 18 times and italic `e^{i…}` 35 times.
   - `microtype` is not loaded.
   - The local Typst is 0.14.2; upstream is 0.15 (June 2026), which adds MathML in HTML export.
