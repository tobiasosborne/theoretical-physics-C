**1. Where the student gets stuck**

- [vl04.tex](vl04.tex:17) says “denken Sie an eine Tabelle”, but no operator is ever applied to a concrete vector until the $3\times3$ example at line 85. Straight after [vl03.tex](vl03.tex:42), which computed everything in $\mathbb C^2$, this is the main break. Fix at equal length: restore the $2\times2$ computation \(\op A\ket\psi=(a_{11}\psi_1+a_{12}\psi_2,\dots)\) from [c03-de.tex](c03-de.tex:1334), paid for by deleting lines 66–71 (below).
- Lines 26–36 define $\op A^\dagger$ only implicitly and assert existence. The first non-symmetric dagger a student sees computed is $\op C^\dagger$ at line 101. One line after line 30 — \(\begin{psmallmatrix}0&1\\0&0\end{psmallmatrix}^\dagger=\begin{psmallmatrix}0&0\\1&0\end{psmallmatrix}\) — would anchor it.
- Lines 59–64: $[\op A]_{xy}$ is named before it is defined, “Entwicklung zweimal einsetzen” replaces the three-line derivation in [c03-de.tex](c03-de.tex:1756), and nowhere is it said that $x$ is the row and $y$ the column. Every later formula (64, 79, 107) depends on that order. Add: “$y$ sagt, welcher Basisvektor hineingeht, $x$, welche Komponente herauskommt.”
- Lines 66–74 abruptly redefine the ket as a map $\mathbb C\to\Hil$, reintroduce an unexplained bold $\mathbf\Psi$, and switch $\Psi/\Phi$ mid-paragraph at line 71. This contradicts the reassurance of [vl03.tex](vl03.tex:7) (“Mehr bedeutet die Notation zunächst nicht”). The web course’s release valve — “Sie können sie jetzt vergessen” ([c03-de.tex](c03-de.tex:1844)) — was cut. Define the ket-bra directly by line 73 and drop the rest.
- Lines 91, 107, 120–126 are verification-free: $\ket\psi$ falls from the sky (only line 130 reveals it is an eigenvector), the ket-bra form of $\op C$ is never applied to one basis vector, and nine eigenvalues are asserted with “und weitere gibt es nicht” before any method exists. One applied check, \(\op C\ket1=\ket0\) from both sides ([c03-de.tex](c03-de.tex:1948)), teaches more than the eigenvalue list.

**2. Kernel or not**

Not essential here: *isometrisch* as a separate class (44), since line 56 immediately collapses it to unitary; *normal* (46, 56, 120), unused until a spectral theorem this chapter does not state; the inner-product dependence of $\op A^\dagger$ (30); hermitesch/selbstadjungiert in infinite dimensions (50); kets as maps $\mathbb C\to\Hil$ (66–71); the whole eigenvalue-loci coda (111–130), which pre-empts the next lecture. That is roughly a third of the chapter.

Missing essentials: operator composition is used from line 34 on ($\op A\op B$, $\op A^2$, $\op B^\dagger\op B$) but never defined as “apply the right one first”, let alone that order matters; the ket-bra multiplication rule $\ketbra{a}{b}\ketbra{c}{d}=\braket{b}{c}\ketbra{a}{d}$, without which line 81’s “komplizierte Eins einschieben” is a slogan, not a skill; and the fact that $\op B=\ketbra{u}{u}$ with $\ket u=(1,1,1)/\sqrt3$ — the one object that ties projector, positivity, ket-bra and later states together.

**3. Against [vl04-v0.2.tex](vl04-v0.2.tex:1)**

Shorter and more correct, not easier. Biggest improvements: (i) the operator is now defined first (9–17) instead of identity and zero preceding the concept (old 7–33), and finite dimension is stated where it is load-bearing (17, 50, 56), fixing the old unqualified “hermitesch (Synonym: selbstadjungiert)” (old 70); (ii) eigenvalues get an informal definition (111) and class-by-class one-way implications (112–120), replacing the old undefined, overbroad “für normale Operatoren überträgt sich … wörtlich” (old 207), and old errors at 121, 156, 199 are gone. Biggest losses: (i) the displayed generic matrices — identity (old 13–22) and conjugate transpose (old 40–52) — plus the $2\times2$ example absorbed from vl03; the new text preaches “Tabelle” and shows none; (ii) worked transparency: the old $\bra\psi\op A\ket\psi$ sandwich was written out in full (old 158–173), the new line 91 precomputes $\op A\ket\psi$ in prose, hiding exactly the step beginners must practise; the old physical glosses “Ereignis oder kein Ereignis” (69) and real expectation values (70–73) shrink to the catalogue sentence at line 48.

**4. False or misleading as written**

- Line 30 says for another inner product $\op A^\dagger$ is generally *not* the conjugate transpose; line 64 says it *is* “Transposition mit Komplexkonjugation”, unqualified. Both need the missing distinction: matrix elements in an orthonormal basis versus entries in $\mathbb C^n$ ([c03-de.tex](c03-de.tex:1792) has it).
- Line 56, “Isometrien sind normal”, is unqualified and false in infinite dimensions — two sentences after line 50 warns about exactly that case.
- Line 48: states are not “positive (mit einer Normierung)” — the invariant is trace one, not a norm; a single projector is a yes/no event, not “die Messungen”.
- Lines 122–126: $\op B$ on $\mathbb C^3$ is listed with eigenvalues “$1,0$”; with multiplicity it is $1,0,0$. As printed, students count two eigenvalues for a $3\times3$ matrix.
- Lines 111–119 plus figure (a) invite the converse reading (real eigenvalues ⇒ hermitesch). Nothing says these are necessary conditions on eigenvalues *that exist*, nor gives the one-line reason $\braket{v}{\op A v}=\lambda\norm v^2$.
- The Merksatz (22) is violated by the chapter itself: kets, bras and ket-bras as operators (66–73) and $\lambda\mathbb I$ (120) carry no hat.
