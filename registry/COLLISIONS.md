# Open notation collisions, for TJO

Each collision is recorded in `registry/notation.json` (`collisions`). Nothing here is settled. Where a smoke chapter needs a symbol now, one meaning is marked `blessed-provisional`; that is a working choice, not a decision.

## COL-F: `F`

| Meaning | Symbol ID | Rendered now as | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Effektoperator / effect operator | sym.effect-operator | F | collision-open |
| freie Energie / Helmholtz free energy | sym.helmholtz-energy | F | collision-open |

Conflict: F is the effect (detector) operator in the script (vl06.tex Postulat 3 and 4) and the Helmholtz free energy in thermodynamics. BRIEF.md P3/P4 write E for the effect, which would collide with energy eigenvalues E_n and with Plavala's E(K).

Proposed resolution: Keep F for the effect operator in Parts I-III and F for the free energy in Parts IV-V as two semantic macros (\EffOp, \Fhelm) with disjoint chapter scopes recorded in the registry; if a chapter needs both, the free energy takes the IUPAC letter A there.

Reason: Both letters are the ones a Lehramt student meets at school and that the existing script uses (vl06.tex); E for effects would collide in every chapter that has an energy.

Provisional for the smokes: none; no smoke chapter needs the quantum effect operator or the free energy (C2 uses Plavala's lower-case f as a bound variable).

Provisional for the smokes: sym.laplacian and sym.simplex are both blessed-provisional; their tokens differ (Δ and Δ_n).
